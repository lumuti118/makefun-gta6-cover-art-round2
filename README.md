Turn your own photos into a Vice City sunset poster — a multi-panel GTA-style cover with bold caption lettering, ready to post. Upload one photo or several, pick a layout, grade it sunset, neon, noir, palms or vice, write your caption, and export a print-ready PNG. Everything runs in your browser and works offline; your photos are read locally and never uploaded anywhere.

## What it does

- **Any photo**: upload your own photos (or try the two bundled samples), fill several panels with one photo or spread several photos across them.
- **Crop and position**: drag a photo inside its panel to move it, scroll on a panel to zoom, or use the per-panel zoom slider and Centre button.
- **Panel layouts**: Hero + 2, Trio, Quad, Full photo, and a Tilted strip of snapshots.
- **Caption**: main caption and sub-caption, size, four lettering colours, top or bottom placement — bold outlined poster lettering with a slight tilt and a gradient fill.
- **Colour grades**: Sunset, Neon, Noir, Palms (with a halftone sun and palm silhouettes), and Vice.
- **Finish**: film grain and a vignette, plus a thin white poster frame.
- **Fast export**: one click exports a PNG in post sizes 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350, or 1920 × 1080.

All artwork is drawn on a canvas with code — an original design with no Rockstar logos, artwork, characters, or fonts. Lettering uses bold system fonts, so the app needs no font files and makes zero network requests. The two bundled sample photos are the kit starter's, made for make.fun with an image model and free to use.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open <http://localhost:8080> in a desktop browser.

No build step, no dependencies. Node 22 is enough.
